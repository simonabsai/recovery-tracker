Your Daily Protocol

☀️ Morning Routine (5 minutes)

Upon waking, still in bed

Alternate between:
  • Physiological Sigh - 5 rounds
    • Double inhale through nose (big breath + small sip)
    • Long, slow exhale through mouth
    • Repeat 5 times
  • Box Breathing - 5 minutes
    • Inhale for 4 counts
    • Hold for 4 counts
    • Exhale for 4 counts
    • Hold for 4 counts
    • Repeat cycle

🏋️ Before Exercise (2-3 minutes)

Before any exercise that previously caused flares

Step 1: Visualization (60 seconds)
  • Close your eyes
  • Visualize doing the exercise smoothly and comfortably
  • See yourself moving without pain or tension

🌙 Evening Routine (12 minutes)

Before bed

1. Meditation (10 minutes)
  • Use Headspace app
  • Choose a beginner or basics meditation
  • Focus on breath, let thoughts pass

  • Consistency over perfection: Do what you can daily
  • Track everything: Use Daily Log to note practices
