Your Daily Protocol

☀️ Morning Routine (5 minutes)

Upon waking, still in bed

Alternate between:
  • Physiological Sigh - 5 rounds
    • Double inhale through nose (big breath + small sip)
    • Long, slow exhale through mouth
    • Repeat 5 times
  • Box Breathing - 5 minutes
    • Inhale for 4 counts
    • Hold for 4 counts
    • Exhale for 4 counts
    • Hold for 4 counts
    • Repeat cycle
Then: Complete your morning check-in in Daily Log tab

🏋️ Before Exercise (2-3 minutes)

Before any exercise that previously caused flares

Step 1: Visualization (60 seconds)
  • Close your eyes
  • Visualize doing the exercise smoothly and comfortably
  • See yourself moving without pain or tension
Step 2: Box Breathing (5 rounds)
  • 4-4-4-4 breathing pattern
  • Calm your nervous system before movement
Step 3: Modified Exercise
  • Do exercise at 70% of usual intensity/volume
  • Listen to your body
  • Stop if it feels wrong

🌙 Evening Routine (12 minutes)

Before bed

1. Meditation (10 minutes)
  • Use Headspace app
  • Choose a beginner or basics meditation
  • Focus on breath, let thoughts pass
  • Don't judge yourself when mind wanders
2. Vagal Exercises (2 minutes)
  • Humming: Hum while brushing teeth or just before bed
  • Cold water: Splash cold water on face for 30 seconds
3. Sleep Prep
  • No screens after this routine
  • Cool, dark room
  • Consistent bedtime

💡 Key Reminders

  • Consistency over perfection: Do what you can daily, even if rushed
  • Track everything: Use Daily Log to note which practices you completed
  • Watch for patterns: Note which exercises trigger 2-3 day flares
  • Total time commitment: ~20 minutes per day
  • Timeline: Expect to see changes in 4-6 weeks with consistency
